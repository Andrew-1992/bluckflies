What we collect

  • Traveller details needed by airlines: full names, gender, date of birth, nationality, and for international trips passport number and expiry date.
  • Contact details: phone number, email address and preferred language, used for your tickets and messages about your booking.
  • Bookings and payments: flights, prices, booking codes, ticket numbers, payment method, amounts, payment references and refund records.   • Voice: if you speak to BluckFlies, your phone's or browser's own speech service turns your voice into text; we receive the text, not a recording.
  • Sign-in and security: one-time sign-in codes (stored only in a scrambled form), and technical records such as your internet address.   • Call-back requests: your name, phone number and message when you ask us to call you.

Why we use it

  • To search for flights, reserve seats and issue tickets with airlines.
  • To take payments, confirm them and make refunds.
  • To send your tickets and important messages about your bookings.
  • To answer your questions and help you through our support team.
  • To keep BluckFlies secure and prevent fraud and abuse.
  • To keep the accounting and tax records the law requires.

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising.

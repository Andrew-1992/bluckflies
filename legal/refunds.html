Cancellations and refunds

Last updated: 5 November 2026

Before you confirm a booking, its card shows whether the fare is refundable and any airline penalty. The rules below explain what happens in each case. Questions: {{email}} or {{phone}}, with your booking code.

Before you pay

  • Reserved seats cost nothing until you pay. You can cancel a reservation at any time before paying, free of charge.
  • If you do not pay by the deadline shown, the seats are released automatically.
  • If you cancel while a mobile money request is waiting on your phone, do not approve it. If you already approved it, you get a full refund.

Payments that arrive late

If your payment reaches us after your booking expired or was cancelled, we refund it in full. Card and mobile money payments are refunded automatically; bank and cash payments are refunded by our staff (see "How refunds are paid").

After your ticket is issued

  • Refundable fares: you can cancel before departure and receive the amount you paid minus the airline's penalty, which was shown before you booked. Some airlines accept cancellations only through a BluckFlies agent; the assistant will connect you.
  • Non-refundable fares: the fare cannot be refunded if you cancel. A change of date may still be possible with the airline's fee and any fare difference: ask for an agent.
  • Return tickets are one booking: a cancellation applies to both directions. To change just one direction, ask for an agent.
  • Unused airport taxes on a non-refundable ticket: [to be reviewed: whether BluckFlies requests these back from the airline on request, and any handling fee].

If the airline cancels or cannot honour your fare

If the airline cannot confirm a fare you have paid for, or cancels your flight, you are entitled to a full refund. We will also help you find another flight. For delays and other disruption, the airline's conditions of carriage apply, and we help you contact the airline.

If the amount paid is wrong

If we receive a different amount from the booking total, we do not issue the ticket. Our staff contact you to settle the difference or refund your payment.

How refunds are paid

Refunds go back the way you paid, in the currency you paid in:

  • Card: to the same card. It usually appears within a few days to a few weeks, depending on your bank.
  • Mobile money: to the same wallet, in South Sudanese pounds: the SSP amount you paid, or the matching share of it for a partial refund.
  • Bank transfer or deposit: by bank transfer. Our staff will ask you for account details and check that they belong to the person who paid.
  • Cash at an office: in cash at the same office, with your receipt and ID, or by bank transfer.
